# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Stilldesk collects on this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Stilldesk is a study app that is **launching soon on Android**. This policy covers our **pre-launch website, waitlist, survey and feedback form**. A full privacy policy for the app itself will be published before the app is released, and it will replace or extend this one.

We built Stilldesk to need as little of your data as possible. We do not show ads, we do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the waitlist and understand what students want.

**Waitlist information.** When you join the waitlist we collect your **email address**, your **name** (optional), and a record of your **consent** (the time you agreed and the version of the consent text). We also record whether you confirmed your email and whether you later unsubscribed.

**Survey answers (optional).** If you complete the short survey, we collect what you choose to tell us: your study year, apps that distract you, when you lose focus, whether you prefer a stopwatch or a timer, what you would pay, what would make you delete the app, your phone type and brand, and whether you want to try the closed beta. All of these are optional. Please do not write sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

**Feedback.** If you send feedback, we collect your **message** and, if you choose to give it, your **email address** so we can reply.

**Email delivery data.** Our email provider tells us whether an email was delivered, bounced or marked as spam, so we can stop emailing addresses that do not work or that complained.

**Technical data.** Our servers and providers process limited technical data needed to run the site securely, such as your **IP address**, browser type and the pages requested. We use the IP address for security and to limit abuse (for example, to stop a bot from submitting hundreds of sign-ups). We do not use it to build a profile of you.

**Site statistics.** We use {{ANALYTICS_TOOL}}, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count visits and button clicks. We do not send your name, email or anything you type in a form to analytics, and we do not use it to follow you across other websites.

## 3. What we do not collect

- No advertising identifiers and no advertising or cross-site tracking.
- No sale or rental of your personal information.
- No accounts or passwords on this website.
- No payment card details (there is nothing to buy on this website yet).
- No task text, study sessions or app data. The app is not released yet.

## 4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Add you to the waitlist, confirm your email, and send you launch, beta and early-bird updates.
- Invite you to the closed beta if you opted in, and choose a mix of phone types for testing.
- Understand what students need so we can build the right product (survey and feedback).
- Reply to your messages.
- Keep the website secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
- Meet legal obligations.

**Legal bases (where laws such as the EU or UK GDPR apply):** your **consent** (waitlist emails, survey and beta opt-in), our **legitimate interests** (security, preventing abuse, understanding site use, answering your messages), and **legal obligation** where required. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## 11. International transfers

Our providers may process and store data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect your information when it is transferred internationally.

## 12. Security

We use HTTPS, restrict access to our systems, store confirmation tokens in hashed form, rate-limit sign-up endpoints and validate all inputs. No method of transmission or storage is 100 percent secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 13. A preview of the app's privacy approach

When the Stilldesk app launches, its design principles are: your data stays on your phone unless a feature needs the cloud (AI brain-dump, study rooms, Pro sync); blocking runs on your device and the list of apps you block is not sent to us; for the AI feature we send only the text you typed, your subject names and your free hours, and we do not log task text; there are no ads and no selling of data; and you can export or delete everything. The app's own privacy policy will give the final, exact details.
