## 2. The plan that disappears (`#problem`)

**Eyebrow:** Why Stilldesk

**H2:** You made a plan. Then you walked to your room.

**Body:**
It happens in a lecture, or in a free period between classes. You think: tonight I will do the DBMS assignment, revise physics chapter 3, and buy a notebook. It feels clear and easy.

Then the walk back happens. By the time you reach your room, the plan has gone soft. You open your phone "just for a minute," and an hour disappears.

That plan did not fail because you are lazy. It failed in the gap between the place where you made it and the place where you needed it.

**Closing line (emphasised):**
Stilldesk is built for that gap.

## 3. The loop (`#loop`)

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**H2:** From class to desk, in six calm steps

**Intro line:** One loop. Nothing to manage, nothing to feel bad about.

**01. Dump**
*Write it messy.*
Type or speak whatever you need to do, in any style, with typos and mixed languages. Stilldesk's AI turns it into tasks sorted by subject, with time estimates and a suggested order. You check the preview and confirm. Nothing is saved without you.

**02. Arrive**
*Your plan finds you at your desk.*
When you reach your room, one gentle notification brings your plan back, in your own words: "You wrote: finish DBMS assignment." Use your saved Wi-Fi, your location, a time, or a simple "I'm at my desk" button. Never a full-screen takeover.

**03. Sit down**
*Two taps to start.*
Your first task is waiting, big and clear. Pick a mode (stopwatch, timer or a 90-minute deep work block), name what you will finish, and press Start.

**04. Focus**
*A quiet screen, a lit lamp.*
The lamp glows while you study. Pause whenever you need to. Ending a session is always one tap. When you finish, you see your time and what you got done.

**05. Protect**
*Only while you study.*
The apps you chose to block show a friendly "Your session is on" screen. The moment the session ends, they open as normal. Outside a session, Stilldesk blocks nothing.

**06. Study together** (After launch)
*Keep each other company.*
Join a private room with friends, or a public topic room. You see small lit windows: who is studying right now. No chat, no rankings, no pressure.

## 7. Blocking, honestly (`#blocking`)

**Eyebrow:** Will it lock my phone?

**H2:** No. Blocking only happens while you study, and you are always in control.

**What Stilldesk does**
- Blocks **only the apps you choose** (for example Instagram or YouTube), and **only during a study session.**
- Shows a friendly screen: "Your session is on," with two buttons: **Back to studying** and **Take 5 minutes.**
- Lets you **end the session in one tap**, from the session screen or from the block screen.
- Gives you a **5-minute emergency snooze** (once or twice per session).
- Opens everything normally **the moment the session ends.**

**What Stilldesk never does**
- It never blocks anything outside a session.
- It never blocks **Phone, emergency calling, messages for emergencies, Settings, Clock, or Stilldesk itself.**
- It never makes you ask permission to quit.
- It never shames you for ending early.

**It's a blocklist, not an allowlist.** Calculator, PDF reader, notes and everything else you did not pick just work. (A strict "allowed apps only" mode is planned for Pro.)

**The honest limits**
- Websites are **not blocked in version 1.** If you want your browser blocked, add the browser app itself to your list.
- A determined person can turn off a permission or uninstall the app. That is fine. Stilldesk is a speed bump you chose, not a cage.

## 8. The Lamp (`#lamp`)

**H2:** Light on means you're studying.

**Body:**
Stilldesk shows your session as a warm circle of light on a dark desk: a lamp. Start a session and it glows on. Take a break and it dims. Finish and it fades out gently.

The lamp is a status, not a score. It never grows, shrinks, levels up or "dies." There are no trees to water and no pet to keep alive. Just a light that tells you where you are.

**Three states shown as small lamps with text labels:**
- **Studying**: steady, warm glow with a very slow breath
- **On break**: the glow dims to about half
- **Paused**: the glow holds, a little cooler

**Small note:** The lamp always comes with words ("Studying", "On break"), never colour alone. In reduced-motion mode and low-battery mode the glow stays still.

## 9. Our promises (`#promises`)

**H2:** Seven promises we build around

1. **Blocking only inside a study session.** Never at any other time.
2. **You are always in control.** End session is one tap. You choose which apps are blocked.
3. **No shame.** No "you failed" messages. One missed day does not erase your progress.
4. **Few notifications.** Two a day at most by default, with quiet hours.
5. **Privacy first.** Your data stays on your phone unless a feature needs the cloud (AI, groups, sync). No ads. We never sell your data.
6. **Fast.** Capture takes seconds. Starting a session takes two taps.
7. **Honest.** We will never say Stilldesk blocks something it cannot.

## 12. Private by default (`#privacy`)

**H2:** Your plan is yours.

- **On your phone first.** Tasks, subjects and sessions are stored on your device and work offline.
- **Blocking never leaves your phone.** The app-blocking check runs on the device. The list of apps you block is not sent to us.
- **The cloud only when a feature needs it.** AI brain-dump, study rooms and Pro sync. For the AI, we send only the text you typed, your subject names and your free hours. Nothing else from your phone, and we do not log your task text.
- **No ads. No selling your data.** Ever.
- **Export or delete everything** from Settings, easily.
